How to play it

The skank: offbeats only

count:  1  &  2  &  3  &  4  &
strum:  .  X  .  X  .  X  .  X     X = short downstroke, choke right after
  • Choke: release fretting-hand pressure straight after each hit so the chord is short and dry.
  • Bubble: add a muted upstroke right after each skank.
  • Neck or middle pickup, treble up, light reverb, no distortion.
