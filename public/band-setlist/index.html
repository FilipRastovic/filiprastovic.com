1. Mi smo najjači

Distorted · hip-hop / rap-rock

Tempo 76 BPM Feel straight 8ths Loop 4 bars ≈ 6.4 s Tone distortion
Rehearsal recording · open file

Structure

Bars 1–2D5 (A string, 5th fret) Bars 3–4A5 (low E string, 5th fret) RepeatSame loop as Paranoja, but straight and heavy

Notes from rehearsal

  • Tempo steady at 76. Good.
  • A D#/Eb shows up in the A5 sections (around 0:13 and 0:26). If it's deliberate, keep it (b5, sounds heavy). If not, check for a slide or a ringing string.

Guitar ideas

Palm-muted chugs, open accents on the snare (2 and 4)

A|-5-5-5-5-5-5-5-5-|
E|-3-3-3-3-3-3-3-3-|   mute, mute, OPEN on 2 and 4

The b5 as the heavy move

A|-------------6---|
E|-5-5-5-5-6-------|   A5 chugs → Bb5 → Eb

Drop D option (low E tuned down to D)

D|-0-0-0-3-0-5-0-6-|   open D chugs + chromatic climb

Unison bend over A5

B|-10----|
G|-12b14-|
  • Pinch harmonics on chug accents; pick scrape into section changes.
  • Killswitch / volume stutter in 16ths on long notes.
  • Stop: all hit D5 on beat 1, a bar of silence under the vocals, then everyone back in.
  • Verses palm-muted or lighter, chorus full distortion, so the chorus actually hits harder.

Structure

D5 / C5 (A string)       A5 / G5 (low E string)
D|-7---5---|            A|-7---5---|

Notes from rehearsal

  • Chord changes land on time.
  • Tempo starts around 77, sinks to about 70 in the middle and recovers to 74. Hold 74 the whole way.
  • G5 barely comes through. Make the G5 hit clearly heard.
  • The C5→D5 move sounds slurred through C#. Slide on purpose or jump cleanly.

Guitar ideas

Ghost strums ("chucks") on 2 and 4

D5  .  x  .  D5 D5 x  .      x = muted strum with the snare

Hammer-on into the chord

A|--3h5--5----3h5--|

Fill at the end of bar 4 (A minor pentatonic)

G|-------5-7-------|
D|---5-7-----7-5---|
A|---------------5-|  → D5

Double-stop stab

B|--5----8-5--|
G|--5----7-5--|
  • Verses: octaves only (A = low E string 5th fret + D string 7th fret). Leave room for the vocals.
  • Chorus: full power chords.
  • Answer the vocal phrases with short licks in the gaps.
  • Tone: neck pickup, tone knob rolled back, optional slap delay (~610 ms, dotted 8th at 74).

3. Mala je luda

Drums and keys · guitar part to be added

Tempo 123 BPM Feel straight 8ths Key A minor / harmonic minor Bar ≈ 1.95 s
Rehearsal recording (drums + keys, no guitar yet) · open file
Tune first. The keyboard is about 30 cents (a third of a semitone) flat. Either the keys player sets master tune to A440 (best), or the guitar tunes to the keyboard's A.

What the band plays

  • Keys hold an A drone almost the whole time.
  • A chromatic line moves above it around E–F–F#–G, with G# showing up a lot.
  • Drums are steady; the tempo doesn't drift.

Guitar part (proposed)

Base: A5 palm-muted 8ths, locked to the kick

A|-0-0-0-0-0-0-0-0-|
E|-----------------|   (or A5 at the 5th fret of the low E string)

Turnaround every 2 or 4 bars: F5 → E5 → A

A|--3--2--0-------|
E|--1--0------0---|

Riff that follows the keyboard's chromatic line

A|-0---0---0---0---|
E|---0---1---2---1-|   E F F# F

Harmonic-minor arpeggio (slow, let it ring, add delay)

G|---------2-1-----|
D|-----2-3-----3-2-|
A|-0---------------|
  • The keys own the middle register. Guitar stays low (chugs) or high (single notes on the B and G strings).
  • No full strummed chords on top of the keys.

4. Letnji je dan a ja sam san nagudran

Punk rock · power chords

Tempo 160 BPM Feel straight, downstrokes Chords C5 G5 A5 F5 Loop 4 bars ≈ 6 s
Rehearsal recording · open file

Structure

LoopC5 → G5 → A5 → F5, one bar each WatchWe speed up to about 170 in the second half. Stay at 160.

Two positions: verse low, chorus high

VERSE  (low E string)      CHORUS (A string)
C5 = 8th fret              C5 = 3rd fret
G5 = 3rd fret              G5 = 10th fret
A5 = 5th fret              A5 = 12th fret
F5 = 1st fret              F5 = 8th fret
  • Verse: palm-muted 8ths, all downstrokes.
  • Chorus: open, ringing chords. This contrast is the song.

5. New song (title TBC)

Keys + drums + guitar · recorded 28 Oct · dark, Spanish/Balkan flavour

Tempo 133 BPM Feel straight 8ths Key A Phrygian (A Bb C D E F G) Bar ≈ 1.8 s
Rehearsal recording, 28 Oct · open file
Tuning again: this recording is ~30 cents flat, same as Mala je luda. It's the keyboard: set master tune to A440 and tune the guitar to 440 too.

What we play

  • Everything sits on A, with a lot of Bb (the half-step above A). That's the Phrygian sound: dark, Spanish/oriental.
  • The bass moves down A → G → F → E (the "Andalusian" descent), and the song lands on E before returning to A (around 0:33–0:41).
  • Tempo starts ~131 and creeps up to ~136 by the end. Small rush; keep it at 133.

Guitar ideas

Descent in power chords (low E string)

E|-5---3---1---0---|   A5  G5  F5  E5

A|-0-0-1-0-0-0-1-0-|
E|-----------------|   chug A, flick to Bb (1st fret) and back

Dominant hit on E (use the G#)

D|-2-|
A|-2-|
E|-0-|   full E major on the last bar before going back to A
